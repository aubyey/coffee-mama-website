Position Overview
The Coffee Mom continuously seeks a friendly and energetic Coffee Shop Attendant to join #TeamCoffeeMom. As a #TeamCoffeeMom Coffee Shop Attendant, you will ensure exceptional customer service, maintain cleanliness, and support daily operations in our bustling coffee shop. Your role will involve taking customer orders, preparing beverages, handling transactions, and creating a welcoming atmosphere for our patrons.

Responsibilities
1. Greet and assist customers in a friendly and professional manner, taking their orders accurately and efficiently.
2. Operate coffee-making equipment, such as espresso machines, grinders, and blenders, ensuring proper functioning and cleanliness.
3. Handle cash and electronic transactions, maintaining accurate records and balancing cash registers at the end of each shift.
4. Maintain cleanliness and organization of the coffee shop, including the seating area, counter, and restroom facilities.
5. Ensure sufficient stock of supplies, including coffee beans, milk, syrups, and other ingredients, and notify management when reordering is needed.
6. Monitor and manage food items, pastries, and other merchandise inventory levels, ensuring freshness and proper storage.
7. Provide excellent customer service, addressing customer inquiries, resolving complaints, and making preference recommendations.
8. Collaborate with team members to maintain a smooth flow of operations, including assisting with food preparation, cleaning tasks, and restocking.
9. Adhere to health and safety regulations, following proper food handling, sanitation, and hygiene practices.

Qualifications
1. Previous experience in a customer service role, preferably in a coffee shop, café, or restaurant setting.
2. Knowledge of various coffee and tea beverages, brewing methods, and specialty drink recipes.
3. Outstanding interpersonal and communication skills. Can engage with customers and work effectively in a team environment.
4. Excellent multitasking abilities and the capacity to work in a fast-paced environment.
5. Attention to detail and a commitment to maintaining cleanliness and hygiene standards
6. Positive attitude, enthusiasm, and passion for delivering exceptional customer experiences.
7. Flexibility to work varied shifts, including mornings, evenings, weekends, and holidays, in addition to the coffee shop's operating hours.
