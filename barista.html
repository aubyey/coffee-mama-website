Position Overview
We are continuously looking for a skilled and passionate Barista to join #TeamCoffeeMom. As a #TeamCoffeeMom Barista, you will be responsible for crafting and serving high-quality coffee beverages to our customers. Your role will involve operating coffee-making equipment, providing outstanding customer service, and maintaining a clean and organized workspace. This is the perfect opportunity if you love coffee and enjoy creating delightful experiences for coffee enthusiasts.

Responsibilities
1. Prepare and serve various coffee beverages, including espresso, cappuccinos, lattes, and specialty drinks, following established recipes and quality standards.
2. Operate coffee-making equipment, such as espresso machines, grinders, and brewers, ensuring proper settings, calibration, and maintenance.
3. Consistently produce high-quality coffee with attention to brewing techniques, extraction times, and milk frothing.
4. Take customer orders, provide recommendations, and offer exceptional customer service, ensuring a positive and personalized experience.
5. Handle cash and electronic transactions accurately, including operating a cash register and processing payments.
6. Maintain cleanliness and organization of the coffee bar, equipment, and seating area, following health and safety guidelines.
7. Monitor and replenish coffee beans, milk, syrups, and other supplies, ensuring optimal stock levels for uninterrupted operations.
8. Stay updated on coffee trends, new brewing methods, and specialty beverages, continuously expanding your knowledge and skills.
9. Assist with food preparation, including pastries and light snacks, and maintain their freshness and presentation.
10. Collaborate with team members to ensure smooth workflow, assist during busy periods, and contribute to a positive work environment.

Qualifications
1. Extensive experience as a barista or in a similar role, preferably in a specialty coffee shop or café setting.
2. Strong knowledge of coffee beans, blends, and brewing methods, with expertise in espresso-based beverages.
3. Proficient in operating coffee-making equipment, such as espresso machines, grinders, and milk frothers.
4. Outstanding communication/customer service skills, with the talent to engage with customers, take orders, and provide recommendations.
5. Attention to detail and a commitment to maintaining cleanliness and hygiene standards.
6. Can work well in a fast-paced environment and handle multiple tasks efficiently and accurately.
7. Positive attitude, enthusiasm, and a genuine passion for coffee and creating memorable customer experiences
8. Flexibility to work varied shifts, including early mornings, evenings, weekends, and holidays, as per the coffee shop's operating hours.
